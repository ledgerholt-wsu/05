The World of Video Games

Video games have become possibly the most popular form of entertainment in the modern world. Starting with simple arcade games and progressing to massive AAA open-world adventure games, video games have grown to occupy almost every genre imaginable, meaning there is a perfect game for everybody.

What is a Video Game?

Video games are an interactive form of entertainmnet that let players control a character, an object, or entire worlds virtually. There are also a variety of ways to play these games like personal computers, gaming consoles, phones, handheld devices, and more.

Why Do People Play Them?

There are many reasons people will play video games like:

  • Entertainment
    • Giving many people a way to have fun and relax.
  • Competition
    • Competetive titles like Counter Strike 2 or Valorant allow for intense competition with other players or against friends.
  • Storytelling
    • Many people think of gaming as the best way to experience stories with many games being awarded for having some of the best stories in media such as Red Dead Redemption 2.
  • Socializing
    • Playing online games with friends is a hobby and past-time that many people take part in, online games can even harbor friendships from all over the country or even the world.
