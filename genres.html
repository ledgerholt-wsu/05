Genres

Video games come in many genres, each one offering a different experience. Some focusing on fast-paced combat while others might focus on survival, exploration, strategy, etc. Many modern titles will also combine genres to create new experiences that appeal to a wider audience.

Action

What is it?

Action games focus on quick reactions, fast movement, and intense combat. These games also rely on players overcoming big challenges

Examples:

God of War, Devil May Cry, Bayonetta

Role-Playing Games (RPGs)

What is it?

RPGs allow players to take control of a character or avatar and develop them throughout a journey. They will often include leveling systems, different equipment, quests, exploration, and many choices for the player. This is also possibly my favorite genre of game.

Examples:

Elden Ring, Kingdom Hearts, The Witcher 3

Shooter

What is it?

Shooter games are combat focused, with the weapon of choice nearly always being a ranged weapon like a gun. There are two main kinds of styles of shooter games like a First-Person Shooter (FPS) or a Third-Person Shooter (TPS). These are often competitive with many esports games being fps titles.

Examples:

First-Person Shooter: Counter-Strike 2, Valorant, Call of Duty
Third-Person Shooter: Fortnite

Adventure

What is it?

Adventure games often focus on puzzles, explorantion, and storytelling. These games are often a second genre to RPGs or action games.

Examples:

The Legend of Zelda, Uncharted, Tomb Raider

Platformer

What is it?

Platformers focus on moving throught differnt levels by jumping between obstacles, platforms, and often defeating enemies and bosses. They are popular but were more popular in the earlier years of gaming consoles.

Examples:

Super Mario Bro., Crash Bandicoot, Spyro The Dragon, Jak and Daxter

Sports and Racing

What is it?

Sports and racing games recreate real or fictional sports. These allow players to control specific athletes, teams, or vehicles from real world sports and events, or fictional ones.

Examples:

Madden, Fifa, Mario Kart, Rocket League

Conclusion

These genres are only a small part of the variety found in the world of video games. Many modern titles combine multiple genres together, such as an action adventure game, making it hard for these genres to cover many titles. Some game platforms have added smaller "tags" to help categorize them better, like Steam, showing just how vast and how wide the variety video games can get. This large variety is a big reason video games are so popular all over the world, every player can find a perfect game for them to play if not a perfect genre for them.
