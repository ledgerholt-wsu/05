My Favorite Game - Kingdom Hearts

Kingdom Hearts is my favorite game because of everything it is. This game is a combination from the famous work of Square Enix's Final Fantasy series and the icnonic work of Disney. Kingdom Hearts is an action RPG adventure with strong gameplay, storytelling, exploration, and it has memorable and amazing characters. All these combine into an unforgettable game that I recommend everyone should experience.

Game Info

  • Game Name: Kingdom Hearts Final Mix
  • Genre: Action RPG
  • Release Date: 2002

Gameplay of Kingdom Hearts

The gameplay of Kingdom Hearts uses real-time combat. This means that the combat is fast and never stops, keeping the gameplay interesting and completely interactive. Players can use comboes, magic, level up, gain and use abilities, and fight along side allies like Donald, Goofy, Beast, Aladdin, and more. These allies always change throughout the worlds you visit, with the exception of Donald and Goofy who follow the player throughout every world you visit.

Visiting the different worlds is another form of gameplay. These are contextualized through the use of the Gummi Ship, a spaceship used to travel between the worlds. When in the Gummi ship the gameplay shifts to a flying shooter style for a brief time, from world A to world B. There is one other major gameplay shift in the world of The Hundred Acre would that consists of five unique mini games.

Story

The story of Kingdom Hearts starts on the world of Desitiny Islands, with Sora, Riku, and Kairi. After a short time getting to know them an apocalypse starts when the Heartless attack. Sora is then transported to a mysterious world called Traverse Town where he meets his unlikely allies and to be friends, and disney icons, Donald Duck and Goofy. The three of them go to other worlds based on Disney movies on a quest to save the worlds from darkness and the Heartless.

Characters

The characters of Kingdom Hearts are one of the most memorable parts of the game. The main protagonist is Sora, a young boy who is the chosen wielder of the Keyblade. He is joined by Donald Duck and Goofy, who are sent by King Mickey to find the Keyblade wielder and help him on his quest. Other notable characters include Riku, Sora's best friend who struggles with darkness, Kairi, Sora's love interest, and many Disney characters like Aladdin, Ariel, and Jack Skellington.

Music and Visuals

The music of Kingdom Hearts is one of its strongest aspects, featuring a mix of orchestral and electronic sounds that perfectly capture the game's atmosphere. The visuals are also impressive, with detailed character models and stunning environments that bring the different worlds to life. One of my favorite aspects of the game is that the bring back many of the original voice actors from the respective movies, such as Alice.

What makes it special?

I think what makes Kingdom Hearts the most special is how much passion and care was put into the game. Since its release in 2002, there are still new things being discovered and almost every time I play through the game I learn something brand new. It is an aspect of the game that I have never seen replicated in any other title.
